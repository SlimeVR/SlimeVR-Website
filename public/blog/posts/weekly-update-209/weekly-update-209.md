## Shipment update <img src="/blog/emoji/nighty_nom-1314209503276699708.webp" alt="nighty_nom" class="discord-emoji" />
**Shipment 14:**
This shipment is currently stuck in the US backrooms (aka Customs). Myself and half the server are refreshing the tracking page all day, so if stuff changes you will hear about it the moment it happens.
Crowdsupply have informed us officially that they expect to start shipping stuff next Tuesday. I still stand by my estimate that S14 orders will arive at your door between now and Sep 15, 2025, 10:00

**Shipment 14.1:**
Chain has been and gone to the cave, leaving behind another 180 Full body sets to be added to the accumulating pile of trackers that has become 14.1. Still to come are the 2000+ trackers that are being assembled into the remaining Deluxe tracking sets that were left out of shipment 14. The team really tried to get this shipment finalized this week, but the date has slipped to early next.

**Shipment 15:**
While S14 is the current hotness, S15 is still in the background slowly building up momentum. Currently, we have the bulk of the main and extension PCB's have been made and are in the process of testing and flashing. All the extension cables are ready to go, too. The current bottleneck is the cases, as the minor mould issue I mentioned a few weeks ago slowed down the production. They will arrive next week, or possibly the week after for tracker assembly.
So far, S15 is still on track for October.

## ICM Modules <img src="/blog/emoji/slime_wow-1341418344544211045.webp" alt="slime_wow" class="discord-emoji" />
The ICM45686 breakout.... **IS NOW AVAILABLE~!** DIY peeps: Order now, party later. So far we have sold 113/6000 and counting...
They are available on the slimevr store at https://shop.slimevr.dev/products/slimevr-mumo-breakout-module-v1-icm-45686-qmc6309?1
(Still can't ship to the USA though, sorry...)
For those of you confused by what this means, this breakout is the tracking part of slimevr trackers called the IMU, and each tracker has one. This IMU is the one official trackers use, and people have been waiting quite a while to get their beans on them.
