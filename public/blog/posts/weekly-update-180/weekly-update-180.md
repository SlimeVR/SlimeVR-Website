## Shipment 10
As usual, Mouser is slow, and there doesn't seem to be any improvements. Both us and CS are trying to get them to move daily, but... **There is a bit of good news though?** They're working on automation system for processing CS orders and it seem to be going well. They don't expect it to be fully operational soon, but hopefully it'll be slowly ramping up soon and we sill see the speed increase? <img src="/blog/emoji/firBless-785674502243090492.webp" alt="firBless" class="discord-emoji" /> Can only dream at that point...
## Shipment 11
As you may know, S11 has been delivered to Mouser. People that were waiting for the remainder of Black and White orders (around 20 people, we're terribly sorry) should be getting them now. The rest of S11 is in queue for after S10, so... yeah <img src="/blog/emoji/nighty_flop-1314209488429121556.webp" alt="nighty_flop" class="discord-emoji" /> Expect it sometime in January if you're in S11.
## Shipment 12
We have S12 on hand, but we are postponing shipping it to Mouser yet. I have updated the numbers for S12 in https://discord.com/channels/817184208525983775/1129107343058153623/1316059164606337107 though.

We will likely ship S12 via ocean instead of air like we used to. We're getting quotes now, and should know more next week. Since there is no rush, it will be more economical and sustainable to ship it by ship 🚢 It shouldn't even be that much longer, maybe 20 days instead of 10.
## Set Upgrades
I know a lot of you are asking when you can buy separate slimes and **upgrade your sets**. The answer is **SOON!** We're currently packing 100 of each of 3 types of upgrades: feet (0+2), hip (1+0) and elbows (2+0) and we expect them to be done by the end of the month, and shipped to Mouser for fulfillment! Of course they will be in the same queue as S10 & S11... So there's that. On the bright side, **we will not sell preorders for upgrades!** You will only be able to get them when they're in stock! <img src="/blog/emoji/firPog-785701297478959104.webp" alt="firPog" class="discord-emoji" /> That's a new for us!
