**The S7 packing is done!** The https://discord.com/channels/817184208525983775/1129107343058153623/1204216527402836042 updated to include all the packed numbers and our order estimates. It will be shipped to Mouser & Crowd Supply early next week! We did overmade some white sets because of a planning error, and it's one of the reasons why this shipment doesn't include all of the orders from 2022. I'm sorry for the mistake and we will try to do better next time. In fact, as soon as we get some parts, we will pack and ship a smaller shipment to cover just the sets that were supposed to be in S7. **It should be mid- or early April, if things go as planed, and so far they are.**

**We're working on the tester update**. One of the steps in making slimes is testing the PCBs. Our old tester has seen better days, so we wanted to make a new one and maybe even two so we could quickly put the PCBs into production after we receive them. Luckily we started it now, because without communicating with us, our PCB manufacturer have changed how the PCBs are panelized - they now come in panels of 20 instead of 10 and arranged differently, which will make our old tester unusable anyway. So now we have less than a month to finish it, and thankfully that should be enough time for it, since we know most of the things already <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" />
