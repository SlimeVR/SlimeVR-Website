## Shipment 12
First of all, my sincerest apologies. I still have no idea what's going on at Mouser and why there are many old orders that haven't been shipped yet. The last update I got from Crowd Supply was that __they went through all orders and either contacted everyone who had issues with the order and ordered more than two weeks ago, or their order will be shipped before the end of the week.__

Since emails get sent the next day, I don't know yet if it's true or not, we should know tomorrow. It looks like Mouser still has ~100 sets on hands, and there are still many orders even from 2024 that haven't been fulfilled, so I hope they are for these orders. I should've had my weekly call with CS today, but it was rescheduled to Monday, so no news yet.
## Shipment 13
**S13 is behind schedule.** It was initially planned to be finished by the end of February, but then moved to next week, so hopefully Chain will finish next week and we can send the week after.

There is a small issue though: we fucked up counting USB cables, and Chain ran out of them. **So most S13 sets will not include a USB cable.** I'm very sorry for this, but hope you can forgive us for that. We ordered new cables as soon as we noticed it, but they're still not here. So we made a decision to proceed without them. I'm sure you all will be more happier to get the sets two weeks earlier than to have an extra very short and almost useless type A-to-C USB cable. If you really really really care for this, email our support after you get your set...
## Shipment 14
There are still [many sets remaining in S13](https://discord.com/channels/817184208525983775/1129107343058153623/1340714876682637332) but even they will be sold out at some point (probably after it arrives to Mouser), so S14 has been in production for some time.

**S14 will be using new V1.2 SlimeVR Trackers** based on ICM-45686 IMU <img src="/blog/emoji/watame_bounce_Nod-854100337090756648.webp" alt="watame_bounce_Nod" class="discord-emoji" /> We're finalizing the firmware for them right now, and they will be no worse than BNO trackers, but slightly cheaper! More details on them closer to when S14 ships, which we expect to be mid-May.
## The Spinny
More work on the spinny is happening <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" />

It's really cool and I can't wait to show you how it works. The mechanics are almost finished, but software needs a lot of work. It's a cool almost scientific instrument that will let us make better trackers by finally QUANITFYING DRIFT. No more measurements of drift by "vibes". Will have a definitive answer on how different IMUs compare to each other, which settings to tune to make them better, and etc etc etc <img src="/blog/emoji/watame_bounce_Nod-854100337090756648.webp" alt="watame_bounce_Nod" class="discord-emoji" /> I'm personally very excited, because having a tool like this was a dream of many years <img src="/blog/emoji/firPog-785701297478959104.webp" alt="firPog" class="discord-emoji" />
