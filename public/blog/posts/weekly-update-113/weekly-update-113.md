We also contacted multiple (more than 10) companies asking for help with the assembly. Some of them responded to us already and are interested, so probably during the next week we will visit them and see if any of them are up to the task on reasonable prices and times.

So, yeah 😅 See ya next week? I will tag updates when I have news on S3 though!
