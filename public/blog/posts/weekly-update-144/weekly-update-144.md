Today we're without pictures, sorry! But with some good news!
## Good news, @ everyone!
We secured manufacturing capacity for the slimes! And we expect it to go *FAST*. Which means that the orders from 2023 will be in assembly soon too, and then 2024 and onwards! <img src="/blog/emoji/slimehug-822833057593294908.webp" alt="slimehug" class="discord-emoji" />

Some numbers:
- We need to assemble and pack ~2500 slimes to finish all orders from 2022. We're doing it ourselves at The Cave, and we're half done, because we started testing new PCBs only this week (it's when they arrived, se up), and we had three people sick most of the week. We expect to finish with 2022 next week and ship S8.
- We need to assemble and pack 28000 more slimes to fulfill all orders from 2023. The assembly company expects that they can do 35k per month starting mid-may. They will also help us with packing. It's likely that we will split the fulfillment in two shipments for easier handling, so **orders from 2023 should start shipping in early June, and be fully shipped by end of June.** __Of course, stuff happens, as you know, so. That's our estimates and plans,__ but we're doing our best to ship as many slimes as we can.
- We need to assemble and ship 14000 more slimes to fulfill all *current* orders from 2024. The assembly company expects to be able to do 20k slimes per months after the initial rush of 35k, so **we should be done with current orders by the end of July** and start making slimes for the future orders.
- We already secured the capacity for new sales, and it can be grown if the sales grow. We already have funding and parts for additional 40k slimes after what I listed, so we'd be working on them after we caught up. And if sales grow, we'd be able to easier increase capacity, since we'd have no backlog.
## Testing
Our main priority right now is to do as much PCB testing as we can, so the assembly company had stuff to assemble. The new tester is *amazing*, but the more we do, the faster we can catch up. **So we're building 1 more right now, and the parts for 2 more are on order.** Since panel of 20 slimes takes 90-110 seconds to test, one person can easily operate 2 or 3 testers, and go through our current 56000 PCBs way faster. We expect the second tester to be done next week, but 3&4 will likely be for the next PCBs batch, because of the parts lead time. <img src="/blog/emoji/slimepcbnom-839133114931347486.webp" alt="slimepcbnom" class="discord-emoji" />
## Shipment 8
These were the good news for S9 and onward. For S8, as I mentioned, we still need to finish assembling and packing some slimes, and we're working on it. We had to prioritize testing, and since people have been sick, assembly was moving pretty slow. As I mentioned, we expect to catch up with S8 by the end of the next week, and ship it the week after.
