**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #73** has arrived for you!

**Here's what's going:**
- Testing jig is in development by me, @Anna | DevMiner and @thebutlah <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbnom-813875289246400542.webp" alt="pcbnom" class="discord-emoji" /> Check out a pic for current design, it should be finished and put into production by tomorrow to be on time for when main boards manufacturing is finished! Then we will have another for BNO085 modules and for extensions. It's a small board that is connected to Raspberry Pi, and a SlimeVR board put on top of it and pressed to the spring-loaded pins, and then some software running on the Pi will do a few tests to make sure that everything works correctly and uploads firmware <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" />

- We received all extension cables to our warehouse! We should also soon receive USB cables and straps. Everything else is in manufacturing and hasn't changed since.
- There have been a cool story when absolutely mad lad @Tony saved SlimeVR power consumption in <#1031451571134922822> thread <img src="/blog/emoji/heh-458306855358169099.webp" alt="heh" class="discord-emoji" /> this was very close and he did good job exactly on time for us to fix the modules and fix the order before the production of BNO085 modules properly started! So nothing wasted (except the first 500 modules that I had before... but I'll find what to do with them later oops. They work, just draw too much power...). Slimes can and do last ~15 hours!
