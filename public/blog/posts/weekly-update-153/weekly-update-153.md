As you can see, we're very busy here in the new Cave! Most of the time. The other times... You know, gotta rest and have fun too sometimes!

The last week we primarily focused on getting through our backlog that piled up while we were moving. Shopify orders, warranties, inventory management, and similar things.

We also finished all Batch 3 testing, so we don't have untested PCBs anymore! The last few k PCBs that we tested this week will be shipped on Monday to Chain for assembly <img src="/blog/emoji/nod-755740147903103016.webp" alt="nod" class="discord-emoji" /> They're hard at work too, assembling trackers and packing sets. We are also packing some sets in the cave! Like remaining white and black.

Speaking of, we got some white and black cases in polycarbonate. I decided to make them slightly transparent to make them more resistant to scratches, and to be able to see the status LED. The black turned out pretty nice, but white is... well, see for yourself. To people that want them, they will be used for warranty replacements. We're getting more black and white PC cases later (white non-transparent), and they will be added to the store later too.
