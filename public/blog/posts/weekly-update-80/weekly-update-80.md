Oh my, ~ That's a very late

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #80**, but is a very exciting one!

**First of all, check the video @Smeltie recorded in our office of some of the stuff going on!** https://youtu.be/NM_0Z_vwtm4

They, @Anna | DevMiner and @Enby thing gonna be okay are doing amazing job of organizing the Cave, and helping with the slimes production :3

Yes, you heard it right, **THE SLIMES ARE IN THE ASSEMBLY PHASE!** Well, mostly... Here are some details:

- **We got all Main Tracker and Extension Tracker PCBs in the Cave!** <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbnom-833469925980110908.webp" alt="pcbnom" class="discord-emoji" />
- **We are finishing tester for the PCBs**, optimizing it for faster and more reliable work pipeline :3
- **We got 7500 BNO modules!** The rest, are sadly not in the production yet, but will be soon™️ The manufacturer is a bit overloaded after their past problems, but hopefully this will not affect us... <img src="/blog/emoji/crossed_fingers_paw-762351521244381235.webp" alt="crossed_fingers_paw" class="discord-emoji" /> <img src="/blog/emoji/bno-968229024805560370.webp" alt="bno" class="discord-emoji" />
- **Our batteries are also here!** <img src="/blog/emoji/spicypillownom-839133143793139733.webp" alt="spicypillownom" class="discord-emoji" />
- **AND CASE SAMPLES ARE HERE TOO!** They look so nice 🥺 The manufacturer sent clear ones, so we're not sure yet how purple will look. There is a small fix that's required - we need to change screw holes diameter, but it shouldn't be a problem. <img src="/blog/emoji/V13Case-998973749342777455.webp" alt="V13Case" class="discord-emoji" />
- Extension wires are here too!

What are we missing still:
- Straps and USB cables are still on a train, and no ETAs so far. Thankfully, they're needed only at the last stage of packing, so we can wait a bit.
- Boxes, instructions and stickers are not done yet. We are finishing the design so that's Soon™️ too, also needed at the end only.
- More BNO modules are still waiting for production, but we at least have all the chips.

**Now we will get to the testing and assembling first 7500 PCBs :3** <img src="/blog/emoji/slimepcbnom-839133114931347486.webp" alt="slimepcbnom" class="discord-emoji" /> This is very exciting and frankly unbelievable, we're here now!
