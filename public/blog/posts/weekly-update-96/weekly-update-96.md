**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #96** let's go-o-o-o~

Yes, I'm very excited! **THE BOXES WILL BE PRINTED TOMORROW!** <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesshaking-843240940263964733.webp" alt="eyesshaking" class="discord-emoji" /> The art for the small box is finished and approved, and the printing scheduled for tomorrow! <img src="/blog/emoji/firPog-785701297478959104.webp" alt="firPog" class="discord-emoji" /> We will try to be there when it happens and, if we can, take a few pictures and maybe a short video.

Now, I don't know if that means the boxes will be ready tomorrow. I'm not sure if it's just start of printing and it will take a few days and then some time to cut them to size and such, but it starts tomorrow! Will tell you more tomorrow, I guess.

The second thing is, **that's only the small box, which is made for 5+0, 5+1 and 5+3 sets**. We really accidentally went overboard with the design so it took a long time, and the other sizes are not ready yet, but they will be ready pretty fast. In the meantime, we will start packing in these boxes. That doesn't mean 5-sets will arrive before 7- and 10-sets, because there are still a few other things that we need to do. Namely...

**At the moment, there are no news about extension cases.** They should be in production yesterday, but we didn't have any updates yet. It's possible they just didn't update us (I will ping them later this week), or they didn't start or smth, either way, no news, but it should be done soon, last time they were ahead of schedule.

**We have the new tester PCBs!** They're slightly off too, but we're working on the rest right now and aim to finish at the start of the next week, and the testing itself should be pretty quick since the new tester will be way faster than the old one, and we will not re-flash already flashed boards, only test missing things.

**The soldering progressing very fast.** We're almost through the first batch's main boards.

**We got samples of the second batch PCBs**, will do some tests this week and order them ASAP.

**We got samples of the second batch of extension wires and ordered them for second and third batches.** They will be slightly different (better). We ordered second and third batches of USB cables too. We will try to order second and third batches of straps this week (just replacing the straps that are still stuck... yuuuup...). Other small things are also coming in, like instruction manuals, screws, battery padding foam...

**So I guess our main hurdle right now is the tester and ordering everything for the second batch.** **__Our aim for this week is to handle this and next week to start packing slimes for shipping!!!__** Let's see if we manage <img src="/blog/emoji/firBless-785674502243090492.webp" alt="firBless" class="discord-emoji" />
