**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #104** is "early" again!

First of all, a few pictures from the assembly. It's going *fast*. Maybe slower than all of us and you would've wanted, but we're doing mad progress and some even worked on weekend (bad idea, but it's very chill in the Cave, unlike in our home which doesn't have AC 😂). We're ~25% done with the first batch, is my guess. We're not counting assembled slimes exactly, and we haven't been packing set boxes the last two days, so I don't have exact numbers yet. We should be over 5000 main slimes assembled.
