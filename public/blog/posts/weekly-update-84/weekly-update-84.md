Hi again we're back with

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #84!**

The last week was "fun" and it culminated in the last post in <#1025118385585004544> where I summarized all the problems we're currently facing and what are we doing to try and fix them. **Since then some stuff got better!** Here's what's new:

* **We received USB cables**. Even though it's a small part of SlimeVR, they were in transit for 4 months. Their arrival means there might still be hope for straps to arrive until the end of February. Even if they will be stuck on the border for longer, as soon as Chinese New Year ends **we will be ordering second batch of straps with air shipping**, and they should arrive before end of February and will be used to cover most of the first batch.
* **We're talking with cardboard manufacturers!** It was a long brain pain to contact them, but we contacted two manufacturers for box inserts and are solving the problems finally! Right now no ETAs but shouldn't take long.
* **Sticker samples are ordered!** Another small part, but another small part that we needed to solve. At least they will be done fast, so no delay here is expected.
* **We are working with another manufacturer for IMU modules!** We will try using local PCB assembly manufacturer to assemble part (16000) of IMU modules. They have slightly better lead time (should be done late February), so this should speed up everything by a bit.
* **Main cases are sent into full production. Extension cases went through design approval**, we sent one fix that needed to be done, so the mold for extensions should be ready soon.
* **Instruction safety manual is finalized**, we're translating it into more languages. It's just something that we have to include to legally sell stuff everywhere, so that's a hurdle we had to work through. Thanks to contributors for lots of help with making it and translating <img src="/blog/emoji/Heart-839953810432589884.webp" alt="Heart" class="discord-emoji" />

**Overall we're trying to finish assembly and send if not all than most of the first batch to Crowd Supply by the end of February.** Let's hope we have less delays and more new ways to solve existing ones! <img src="/blog/emoji/notheh-820155473839128596.webp" alt="notheh" class="discord-emoji" />

Thank you for your patience <3
