Hello there, <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" /> Trying again to get back into Mondays with today's

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #88!** A few exciting things are happening~

- **DIY KITS ARE READY TO BE SHIPPED!** And yes that's the first look at our (almost) box design~ The art will be featured on the box of other sets too, but for DIY the art is on the envelope. And since the envelope isn't intended to be reused, we also giving one as a bonus <img src="/blog/emoji/blobsquee-711842986589290576.webp" alt="blobsquee" class="discord-emoji" /> The kits are tested, packed, labeled and ready to be shipped, which will probably happen tomorrow. **They will still take a bit of time to get to backers:** since we started as an Estonian company, they will have to go to USA through Estonia... I know, it's ridiculous, considering some of them will go back to Europe, but that's not something we can change for the first two batches. **We're sending 25 DIY Kits. That's all DIY Kits orders up until February 6, 2023!** If you ordered after, your order is in second or third batch.

I know that's a drop in the bucket, but I'm very excited to start! <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesshaking-662674837558525968.webp" alt="eyesshaking" class="discord-emoji" /> This will also serve as a test of shipping, logistics, customs clearance, etc etc before we start sending bigger shipments. So let's hope all goes well!

- **Extensions cases mold is finished!** The manufacturer will make samples tomorrow and send them to us ASAP. Can't wait! <img src="/blog/emoji/V13Case-998973749342777455.webp" alt="V13Case" class="discord-emoji" />

- **Boxes are almost finalized!** We did a bunch of stress-tests for the boxes, and they are good, except the Deluxe Tracker Set, which is a bigger box that houses a lot of trackers, and as a result didn't survive rough treatment. Box manufacturer says they have ideas on how to improve the insert, so let's hope they'll solve it fast! <img src="/blog/emoji/blobsquee-711842986589290576.webp" alt="blobsquee" class="discord-emoji" /> Meanwhile, **we're finalizing the box art for the template of the boxes!** As with the DIY Kits, and stickers, box art is made by @Flarchik and features our mascot - Nighty. <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" />

- **Extension tester is done and we're now testing extension boards too!** Testing them is so so much faster ha-ha <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbheh-943141381310926858.webp" alt="pcbheh" class="discord-emoji" />

- **16000 IMU modules in production!** They should be ready soon and we will have way more things to solder 😄 One of the major problems solved??

That was a fun week, hopefully the new one will be even more fun, stay tuned and thanks for your support <img src="/blog/emoji/Heart-839953810432589884.webp" alt="Heart" class="discord-emoji" />
