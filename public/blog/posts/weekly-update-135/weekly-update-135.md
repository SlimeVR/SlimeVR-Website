### S7 is being packed!
I updated the https://discord.com/channels/817184208525983775/1129107343058153623/1204216527402836042 to reflect currently packed numbers. We received slimes from the assembly company and continuing to pack them. We expect S7 to ship sometime mid-march if everything goes well.
### All parts for Batch 3+ are ordered!
We ordered the rest of the parts that we didn't have before, including new cases made of Polycarbonate, new Main Boards Revision 14, more batteries, more straps, more BNO! It's all either in production or on the way. We will still need to order a couple hundred more of stickers, boxes, manuals etc but that has very short lead time, so we will order them closer to when we start running out.

We also received the straps that were lost for so long, and now our Cave is full of destroyed boxes of straps <img src="/blog/emoji/sweats-932961011068198912.webp" alt="sweats" class="discord-emoji" /> So we've been doing a lot of cleaning, reorganizing and making new shelves to put everything on its place.

We're also talking with the assembly company about increasing their capacity so they're ready for when the parts for the next batch arrive, and we're looking into another assembly company for more help, since we'd need to assembly 56000 slimes next!
