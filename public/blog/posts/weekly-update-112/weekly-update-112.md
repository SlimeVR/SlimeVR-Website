Hey slimeys ,

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #112** is here with you on this Wednesday <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" /> Let's see what we have here...

First, **Shipment 3**. As of now, it's status still "CUSTOMS HOLD OR INSPECTION", last updated yesterday August 22nd. From what I understood by emails I had with UPS like a week ago, it's been on hold by customs so that FCC can check it. They promised to keep me updated if there is anything that's needed from me. I can assume it's still just in the queue for inspection or something. The good part of that is that we aren't alone in this. Mouser, a very huge USA electronics company which owns Crowd Supply and does all the logistics for us, is also very interested in getting Slimes safely to you, and they're in the loop too. So, I'm going to trust the experts from Mouser and UPS and try to stress less about it.

It looks like they shouldn't hold it for more than 30 days without a justifiable cause, which expires on August 31st, so at least we can hope for an end date on this issue?

The **Shipment 4** is being assembled still. Even if we finish it, we will not send it out until the customs saga is over, out of caution not to get into it again. If there are some things that we would need to do with it after S3 is clears to improve S4 speed, we'd better do it before sending it out.

As you might have noticed in <#1129107343058153623>, and as people discussed everywhere, **shipment 4 pacing slowed down substantially this month.** This is, of course, not planned. A bunch of stuff lined up, most of which personal, so multiple people weren't (and some still aren't) available to work at all or to work on the assembly during August. This includes Poly and I, which is why managing this to solve it wasn't possible. And since we're a small team like that, if 2 out of 4 people can't work, then the pace is like a half or less. And solving this quickly is challenging, since hiring new people isn't easy especially if we are not sure about the work load in the next couple of months.

We're working on that though. Poly is looking into outsourcing all or part of the assembly to local companies, which will give us a lot of flexibility and hopefully speed up the process. We had a meeting with one of them this morning, they will get back to us if they can help us or not. Meanwhile, we're also just getting back into it.

And we have a small bunch of slimes that are assembled, but not yet packed.

**We also have been busy with warranty issues**. As Slimes were delivered, issues arouse. That is unavoidable, but the percentage of problems is pretty high because of a multiplication. Each of you have at least 5 trackers, so if 2% of trackers are broken, it means there is like 10% of people with broken trackers... So we've been dealing with that too.

**We started packing replacements and slowly sending them out.** Missing or broken extension cables and extensions are faster to send out, because we had packaging for them. Packaging for single trackers arrived just recently, so it's still work in progress. Our mail point doesn't accept more than 25 parcels from one sender per day (fuck if i know why <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_a-847203539352551544.webp" alt="nya_a" class="discord-emoji" />), and making labels by hand for ~100 people is a lot of work, so sending them will take us well over into the next week (pic 3 the pre-packed single trackers).

Thankfully, at least reports about broken slimes stoped. Of course, extension cables will continue to break, because they kinda suck... <img src="/blog/emoji/aki_flop-908156245079363594.webp" alt="aki_flop" class="discord-emoji" /> But I'll make a separate post about them below this one, stay tuned! <img src="/blog/emoji/heh-458306855358169099.webp" alt="heh" class="discord-emoji" />

Not sure if I forgor anything, but yeah, that's current news! Oh right, some pictures!
