## Further production
We've received all the money required from Crowd Supply to order all the missing parts, as well as parts for current and future orders. A lot of it already on order, and right now we're doing tests of the new PCB and new case material.

New cases are made from Polycarbonate (PC) and seem to be both more reliable and also prettier! (Top case on the photo - new, bottom - old) If our testing deem it insufficient, we will make a new case design. We should now have enough experience and spare cash to make a new better mold, or even update the old one. For now we hope that we won't need it... <img src="/blog/emoji/V13Case-998973749342777455.webp" alt="V13Case" class="discord-emoji" />

We recently posted the design and schematic of the new main tracker PCB (Revision 14) in https://discord.com/channels/817184208525983775/932235452558245918/1198351417430265896 so if you know stuff about electronics desigh, check it out! We're open for comments before we finalize design <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbnom-833469925980110908.webp" alt="pcbnom" class="discord-emoji" />
