**Dev Mondays-ish Update #105** is on Monday now, wow!

So, baiscally, we're making slimes <img src="/blog/emoji/firL-785677220093231134.webp" alt="firL" class="discord-emoji" /> Here's a few pictures...

Just pure grind <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesshaking-662674837558525968.webp" alt="eyesshaking" class="discord-emoji" /> We're like... ~30% done of the first batch (assembled and packed into shipping boxes) 😢 yeah it's way less than we hoped, but that's the best we could do. We're trying not to hurt our wrists and backs, we're getting help from friends, it's just... takes time. The first batch is really big, we should be able to catch up soon. It doesn't help that it was very hot for some time, and 2 electric screwdrivers broke because of the amount of screws we had to screw in...

The slimes were not designed with the thought of fast and easy assembly in mind. We might update the design for batch 5 or so, tbh... Any way, we're assembling and packing!

We plan to ship what we have next week, still waiting responses from transport companies. I think it will be at least 60% of the first batch, but maybe less. Of course, we will continue assembling the rest and move to the second one right away. So I think the first people will start getting their slimes in around a month. At this point I can say it because we already have a lot of slimes assembled and packed and they are just waiting to be shipped. Depending on responses from transport companies, we might ship some this week (would've been really nice, tbh, we're running out of space in the Cave).

So yeah. On one hand, exciting. On the other, aaaaa... there is so much left to assemble! At least it's just... work. Pretty satisfying work, actually. Making slimes and putting them in boxes is a really nice experience.
