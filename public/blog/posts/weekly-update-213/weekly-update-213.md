## Shipment update <img src="/blog/emoji/nighty_nom-1314209503276699708.webp" alt="nighty_nom" class="discord-emoji" />
**Shipment 14:**
So the rollercoaster ride that is Shipment 14 is nearly over. With the overdue news that Crowdsupply has fixed the bottleneck in their warehousing, we are told the remaining orders "will be shipped today and tomorrow". I hope everyone who was tall enough to ride enjoys their slimes!

Missing straps: All the labels for these were painstakingly printed and are on the way to Chain now. We expect Chain will finish and ship them out to affected customers early next week. Affected customers will be able to see their tracking number **late next week** in their Crowdsupply order page.

**Shipment 14.1:**
Stuck at customs... who could see this coming? Hopefully it gets through soon but its just a waiting game now while UPS and their customs friends battle it out. Fingers crossed it gets out soon.

**Shipment 15:**
Some good news! We now have all the pieces of ~~exodia~~ the trackers. Chain is now assembling tracker sets! Half the cases are en route to chain from the manufacturer, but that shouldn't slow down assembly as this shipment is enormous. It's all down to how fast Chain can assemble everything, now.

**Shipment 16??**
So with shipment 15 being effectively sorted, S16 begins its metamorphosis from fiction to reality. Talks with mouser have already begun, expect this shipment to be big.

## Ecosystem Updates <img src="/blog/emoji/nighty_hug-1314209493747241011.webp" alt="nighty_hug" class="discord-emoji" />
* **Server:**
As previously covered by Aura, 0.16.3 was just shot into the release-o-sphere and is available to download. This version fixes a whole slew of issues that have been annoying everyone for a long time, most notably the foot snapping bug and Native Android HID support being integrated.
This means not only are haunted feet a thing of the past, but those with smol trackers (this includes butterflies) can now plug their dongle in to any android device to serve the trackers. This includes most standalone headsets!

But wait, there's more! We also included is fixes for plugging trackers into android devices, so plugging your tracker into an android device should now be a fun experience instead of a sisyphean feat that tests your patience.

As usual, updating is recommended if you havn't already... ***especially so*** if you are on Android as you can get it direct from the Play Store now~! https://redditez.com/r/SlimeVR/comments/1o5iz37/slimevr_is_now_available_directly_from_the_play/
* **Installer:**
A new version of our installer is being pushed for release in the next few days/weeks that includes a bunch of small fixes, including errors for when the driver doesn't install correctly and unzipping fails, as well as a fix for the pesky elevation issue that required people installing or updating to close and reopen SlimeVR for it to work in VR. These fixes should make installing and updating much smoother, so look forward to seeing this very soon.
* **Driver:**
SteamVR Driver v0.3.1 has officially launched. It patches a SteamVR configuration clash that was causing rare problems and crashing in some users. If you want to get it, just run the installer again and choose update or repair.
## Butterfly News 🦋
Butterfly trackers are coming along fantastically. The design is nearing its final form, which means a lot of other parts can now be organised, like the box, dock, etc. Expect to see lots of cool new stuff in the coming weeks on this as the team starts firing on all cylinders.

Additionally, the core team has been focusing on expanding the communication protocol to make it much more reliable. In tandem with revisions to our dongle, we are hoping to have the best smol dongle available. The changes also give more control over the trackers which allows us to handle some of the tracker functions from the server directly. Amazing stuff~!

The team is really focused on making sure these little guys are extensively tested and really put through their paces in a variety of use-cases. To that end, we also have a bunch of review sets being cooked up that are slated for shipping out early November.

With all that said, the current target is **December 1st for campaign start**.... *Maybe*... Later than we hoped but better late than never. I know I say this every time, but if you are at all interested, please sign up for campaign updates now at https://slimevr.dev/smol It helps us out more than you might think <3
