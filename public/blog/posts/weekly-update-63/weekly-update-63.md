The modules we have are a bit different than what we're used to, so it needs a bit soldering and tinkering to make it work, and I plan to do it this week. <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbnom-833469925980110908.webp" alt="pcbnom" class="discord-emoji" /> Will let you know with a separate update if we make it work, and then we'll try doing testing for drift and such.
