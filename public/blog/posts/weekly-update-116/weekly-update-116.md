Well , quick one today (it wasn't quick).

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #116** is again on Wednesday <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_a-847203539352551544.webp" alt="nya_a" class="discord-emoji" /> maybe we should just move it to Friday, so it's like "end of the week" news? or to Saturday, so I can post it and then have weekend rest? anyhow, what we have right now:

1) **Shipment 3 is going back to the cave.** The storage cost in the warehouse there is absurd at $75 USD and we already racked up $3000 during the time customs and FCC had it... yes we have to pay for it. I know, I also think it's weird... As @𝓶𝓲𝓼𝓸𝓷𝔂𝓪𝓱🌸 mentioned in https://discord.com/channels/817184208525983775/1150902162591850557 "it's called demurrage/detention/storage costs. Normally ranges from $75 to $300 a day" At least we're on the low end I guess!

So it means Shipments 3, 4 and 5 will be bundled together and sent again when we deal with the certifications.

2) **On the certifications.** We reached out to 5 different companies, and we got response from one of them (our first choice and one we got recommended). We're still talking with them the details (why stuff can't be done immediately? 😢), but on the phone (I made a phone call 😭) they said they should have free lab time in October? So hopefully we can do all the testing soon, and then I have no idea how long actually getting FCC ID takes... and then new labels... and putting them on. Basically, **I don't think S3, S4 and S5 will ship before mid-November.** Honestly, would be very lucky if it does, then we can hit the Crowd Supply's "November 30" delivery time that a lot of you see in your order... I don't believe we're that lucky though, so I would bet on Christmas for these shipments.

To clarify, since S5 is not in <#1129107343058153623>, S5 includes all orders up until May 15th 2022, so all the "first batch". We're actually close to it being done in terms of assembled slimes, so it might be way bigger...

Meanwhile I looked up the numbers and how the certification is done and everything, and I don't think we will have any problems with it, just need to do testing and submit paperwork, I believe slimes should be compliant with all related requirements. If they are deemed "too powerful" though, we can lower transmit power somewhat, so it should be fine. They aren't an iPhone...

3) **On assembly** we are currently in contact with 2 companies that are interested (1 more we think won't follow up with us), and today we sent samples to one of them. We're also upgrading our tester which should streamline the testing process so the assembly companies can do assembly while we do soldering, testing and packing without too many bottlenecks.

4) **We are still investigating warranty issues**. We gonna X-Ray some slimes, and I have a bunch of tests planned too, but was busy with... *all this*. Sooo yeah.
