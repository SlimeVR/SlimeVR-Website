Most of this week was spent testing more PCBs for the assembly, packing pallets for the assembly, sending stuff to the assembly company and so on.

We have sent 3 pallets of stuff to them last Friday, and will send more next week. Right now we asked them to pack 3000 sets for S9, but hopefully they can do more, but we're limited by the lack of set boxes, which are in production right now. All of 2023 orders are about 5000 sets, so 3000 is the bigger part of it. Depending on their packing and assembly speed, and how boxes arrive, we hope they can do more in the next couple of weeks. Stay tuned.

For S8 we assembled and packed around half of the slimes and sets needed for it right now, but as I mentioned, priority was to send more stuff to the assembly company, since they can do assembly and packing way more efficient.
