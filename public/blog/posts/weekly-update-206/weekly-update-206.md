## Butterfly Update <img src="/blog/emoji/nighty_hug-1314209493747241011.webp" alt="nighty_hug" class="discord-emoji" />
SlimeVR Butterfly Tracker campaign is steadily approaching, and I want to keep highlighting the cool work that is being done to make these tiny trackers so interesting and unique. *cough cough* go sign up at https://slimevr.dev/smol

I have been constantly pestering the cave for pictures and videos of the alternative mounting choices for those wanting a bit more from their tracker mounting than just velcro straps. I ***finally*** have some pictures and videos for you this time, yippeee~! <img src="/blog/emoji/SKC_yippie-1192868297713135626.webp" alt="SKC_yippie" class="discord-emoji" />
* Clip-on adaptor: This addon is a little clip that you slide the butterfly in to to convert it into a clip-able SlimeVR tracker. There may or may not be different types of these, but the aim is to get it clipable onto common articles of clothing people use in VR (at least id hope), such as pants, socks, shoes, etc
* Clothing: The team is experimenting with a few different clothing options with dedicated mounting spots on them, like crop-tops and thigh high socks (actual good ones, too). These will require a bit more experimentation to get right, so will probably be a later addition, but it shows what is easily doable with iron and sew-on patches and a dream.
* Strap clips: There is lots of experimentation being done to work out the perfect clip system for the tracker straps. I am sure a lot of you are sick of getting the straps on and off, so the aim is to make donning and doffing them as simple as possible

Keep in mind these are all prototypes, so very work-in-progress and may change entirely (or not even become a thing).

Lastly, Smeltie has been working on an adaptor for the Spinny™ to attach a Butterfly inside that will be used to train our custom tracking system specifically for Butterfly trackers. Pic below, but you might get to see it live in action very soon, because...............
