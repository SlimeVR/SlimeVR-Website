Heyo sorry for being late 😄

**Mondays-ish Dev Updates #47**

Ain't much big happened last week. Mainly was fighting with the bank so they let SlimeVR be <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_a-847203539352551544.webp" alt="nya_a" class="discord-emoji" /> I fixed it, all's good, don't worry, SlimeVR is not affected by the world state, beside the mental health of all of us...

Another important thing was fixing the BNO085 module design. My old one that I posted last week about wasn't possible to manufacture with the assembly house we chose (castellated hole too close to the edge is apparently impossible, didn't know this <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbheh-943141381310926858.webp" alt="pcbheh" class="discord-emoji" />), so I had to redesign it in new constraints, which resulted in, in my opinion, pretty cool module, that is easy to solder to SlimeVR main boards, but also universal and compatible with other projects <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbnom-833469925980110908.webp" alt="pcbnom" class="discord-emoji" /> See pics for details. Along with it I ordered new PCB test designs. <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" />

Oh, one other cool thing is that we were sent 1000 chips more! They should arrive in the next couple of days <img src="/blog/emoji/firPog-785701297478959104.webp" alt="firPog" class="discord-emoji" /> They will go in production after first 500 returns and I confirm everything works.

Work on the first batch continues! <img src="/blog/emoji/slimenom-663823059920224277.webp" alt="slimenom" class="discord-emoji" />
