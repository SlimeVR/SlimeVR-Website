**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #86** time! Couple days late, but welp~

**First of all, look at them Slimes!** <img src="/blog/emoji/slimenom-663823059920224277.webp" alt="slimenom" class="discord-emoji" /> We got a (not so) fancy lightbox and made a few new pictures :3 They will be up on Crowd Supply soon too.

Then, **A BOX!** More precisely, a prototype of it! The local box manufacturer was very fast on making some samples for us, and we could experiment with them and see what works and what doesn't, and sent them our changes proposals. So, **the packaging in the works and should be done by the end of the month!** <img src="/blog/emoji/box-874775974024200272.webp" alt="box" class="discord-emoji" /> It should be nice :3

Next, **the sticker samples are here!** And actually all stickers should be here in a couple days! We also have **first samples of safety manuals** that will be used for DIY sets, and the full run of them will be in production soon.

**On the straps**: they're still stuck on a train, but. I was ready to order new batch with air shipment right when I found there is a local company that can make them. So I messaged them and we're discussing it right now. I don't know the price or lead time yet, but if they're not too much pricier and can deliver faster than the Chinese supplier, I would very much rather work with them. As with the boxes, working with local companies is very amazing because it's way easier to cooperate, get samples, and no shipping times.

**On the modules**: We should have boards and parts this week and will send them to local company for them to make next 16000 BNO085 modules. We should go through the 7500 that we have even before the new ones arrive.

**Main cases are in production and should be ready by mid-February!** <img src="/blog/emoji/V13Case-998973749342777455.webp" alt="V13Case" class="discord-emoji" /> If everything arrives but extensions cases are delayed, we will try ship Lower-Body sets ahead of everything.
