This **Dev Mondays-ish Updates #37** is for , & @everyone!

**If you haven't seen yet, there is a new update on Crowd Supply: https://www.crowdsupply.com/slimevr/slimevr-full-body-tracker/updates/progress-and-schedule-updates**

It talks about production delays due to chip shortages (same info I posted in update 36), as well as details on the plans for the new year. Please check it out if you want to know more. If you want to discuss chip shortages in one place, there is a thread: "Chip shortages 2".

Once again, I'm sorry for the delays, I did what I could, and I'll continue to doing all in my capacity to deliver the slimes to everyone, but the world is lacking chips right now, as I'm sure you have heard from everywhere already.

Some other fun stuff that happened this week:
- All my tools, workshop and other stuff is on its way to me! Should arrive in a couple of days. Will be kicking it off with testing and releasing new firmware and server.
- I received new IMUs for DIY slimes that we talk about in "ICM & LSM IMUs support", support for them should make DIY better and more accessible (buying $70 Bno085's is a bad idea, don't do it...)
- **I ordered $50,000 USD worth of other chips** <img src="/blog/emoji/notheh-820155473839128596.webp" alt="notheh" class="discord-emoji" /> Most of it is 22000 of ESP-12F's. Others are 22000 of each of CH340N and TP4056. These should arrive to the PCB manufacturer's factory in a few weeks. Shouldn't have any delays. After they arrive we'll start doing test runs without IMUs to test the board and manufacturing process.
- I'm working out on other parts of production management: working out how it'd be better to ship all produced Slimes to Crowd Supply without paying over $100k USD in tariffs... Maybe we'll move manufacturing to Europe after all. This right now is the most important thing to figure out (I'll share more next time).

Happy New Year everyone, let's make 2022 good together! <img src="/blog/emoji/slimehug-822833057593294908.webp" alt="slimehug" class="discord-emoji" />
