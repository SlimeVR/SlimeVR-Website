Hi ! Sorry for being late (it will happen again).

The **Dev Mondays-ish Updates #93** is here and we don't have anything too cool to share this time.

We're progressing a bit slower than we hoped, mostly because we have a lot of stuff to do before we can simply focus on soldering and assembly and don't think about anything else.

For example, here in the cave, @Uriel and @Enby thing gonna be okay are working on **greatly improving the UI of the Server** to make it way more clear, easier to use and set up. The server has pretty much everything you would need, but some things are not clear or easy, especially for a new user. In preparation for many many users without technical experience, as well as reviewers without much time on their hands, to get the trackers, we're reworking some stuff so people make less mistakes, understand what they need to do, and what to use, and how to make their tracking the best it could be.

@Vyolex and I are working on assembly and testing process.

For example, we need to mark each tracker with a certification symbols (CE, UKCA, WEEE, FCC, TELEC and other scary letters...), **so we're working on a stamp** to do this easily during the assembly process so it takes little time and with little mistakes. Some of the sample photos are attached. It's a small thing but we need to do it for legal reasons 😢 Nailing the design of the stamp and fixture for it requires some experimenting and iteration, which can be tedious. Final stamp will be made with a durable ink that we already have.

**Another thing that we're working is a new tester for SlimeVR main boards.** We have found out that the charging circuit wasn't tested well by the previous tester, so the new tester will do better job at it and should weed out some broken slimes from potentially getting to backers. The new tester will also test a whole panel at a time - 10 boards, instead of one. This should speed up the testing process significantly, so right now we stopped testing new slimes while the new tester is being built (we have more than 7000 tested and awaiting assembly, and we continue soldering new ones for when the tester will be ready).
