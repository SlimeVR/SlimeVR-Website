But there are a lot of things I want to share with you today!

## FCC

First, **FCC certification is going well!** As you can see above, we found a company eager to work with us. They're also from the USA, so it should make everything a bit faster. I already sent them the a set of slimes, which should arrive in the next day or two, and I'm preparing the documentation that we will need to submit to FCC along the test result.

Of course the big problem is that the cost of certification will be around $20000 USD... See screenshot of a part of the quote... That's why we didn't try it before, because if we were correct that we don't need it, these money was something we desperately needed to stay afloat.

Because of this, and the fact that in general our operational costs and parts costs are way higher than what we planned when we started the project, and because we need to keep the stuff going to not only finish all current orders, but also bring you new and cool stuff while paying people working with us full-time at The Cave, and part-time contributors:

## The price of SlimeVR will increase.

**This will obviously happen only for new orders. __No current preorders or orders placed before we change the price will be affected__**. And I'm not exactly sure when we will change it, we will talk with Crowd Supply. When it happens I will make a separate post with explanations and justifications, but that's something we've been talking internally about for months now, but I didn't want to do it. **I also never want to create a sense of urgency for people to order slimes now in fear of the price increase**, but I think announcing it in advance is better than not...

## It won't be much! SlimeVR will keep being the most affordable FBT and the best FBT for its price.

More details when we crunch more numbers and talk with CS.

## Assembly Outsourcing

We're waiting this week more info from the company that Poly and Smeltie went to last week, they should finish trial runs soon.

## Other Cool Stuff

Since we're working on FCC, we actually already have a new FCC ID, so **we ordered new labels**. Which means soon we will start labeling new sets with the actual proper label, and we can relabel the old ones before actually going through the certification. I didn't know we could do that, so that's a nice surprise! **We also need to order new instruction manuals**, because there is stuff we need to include in them. I will try to finish them this week and order them.

**We started working on new main board design**. The next version should include protection from static damage and protection from plugging in USB cable into extension port. This will take a bit of time, and before it's done we will not order new main boards. We still have a few thousands not finished though. Current ones (and new ones too) will get a nice cap for the extension port to protect it 😄 We're currently working on it too...
