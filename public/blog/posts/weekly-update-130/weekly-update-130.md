## We're shipping slimes next week!

We decided to finish repacking S3 & S4 and pack some more of S5 before we ship, and we're very close to finish it, so we've been crunching in The Cave with @Enby thing gonna be okay in the last few days. I bet no one wants another 3 months round-trip because of something that's fully in our control, right? Anyway it's progressing quite well.

We have 1.5 pallets to replace manuals and ~1.5 pallets to update and repack, and we will be done with it next week.
