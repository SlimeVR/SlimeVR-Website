I bet you didn't expect it today, ? Well! It's time to try something new. **Dev Mondays-ish Updates** is no more! Welcome to..

**Weekly Dev Update #120!**

The name changes, and the time changes: now updates will primarily come out on Fridays, not Mondays-or-whenever. The rest stays the same, I will keep bringing you weekly news of SlimeVR production, development and cool stuff! Mondays-ish updates started when SlimeVR was in early stages of development and (as I remember it) was more to keep track of plans for the week. Since we've been in a different pacing for a while, it now makes more sense to keep updates what they become - a rundown of what happened during the week and maybe some plans.

So yeah, let's get to it~

## FCC

A few tests were already done by the lab, and **we are compliant with all the stuff** <img src="/blog/emoji/party2-1122906449358626867.webp" alt="party2" class="discord-emoji" /> In fact, we have a lot of headroom, the RF levels are like 3 times lower than what's allowed. So we need to finish some documentation to go along with our application, and we will submit everything to FCC. I hope we can do that at the start of the next week, stay tuned <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" />

## Assembly Outsourcing

The company should've done trials yesterday, but we still didn't hear from them. In the meantime, we have like 2000 tested boards ready for them to make into slimes. And we also have 25 boxes of made slimes (each is ~125 slimes), but they aren't packed yet. Since we are outsourcing the assembly, we will focus on testing and packing.

## New Tester

We got a PCB for a new tester version! I will assembled it today or tomorrow, and hope it works really well, because it will speed up the testing process a lot <img src="/blog/emoji/firPog-785701297478959104.webp" alt="firPog" class="discord-emoji" /> This is a very complicated board mostly designed by @cake32 <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbnom-833469925980110908.webp" alt="pcbnom" class="discord-emoji" />

## New Main Board Revision

First test iteration of a new main board revision is on order with JLCPCB. Hope we'll get it in a week or so and can test the changes, see if it works and how it handles the static damage and such. The work continues on it, we wanna do some more changes.

Some people asked before what orders will get a new revision. **Only orders assembled after our current boards are out will get new boards.** This might be a bummer for some, but please remember that **they are functionally identical**. They just in theory should be harder to break. **Either one is covered by 2 year warranty since you receive your set, so you have nothing to worry about.** Also all orders that we will relabel anyway will get a plug for extension port to protect it from static damage in a simple way.

So yeah see ya next Friday! <img src="/blog/emoji/owlwave-586779817106604051.webp" alt="owlwave" class="discord-emoji" />
