* **Our case design was accepted and mold manufacturing started!** bu-u-ut...
To the less good news:
* Stuff is taking a long time, and delays are happening everywhere. Here's current problems:
* Case design was accepted with a few iterations of corrections, which took a few back-and-forth's between us and manufacturer, so they started mold only a few days ago. **Their plan is for mold and samples be ready mid-December.** (we aren't alone who needs injection molding...) Factoring in full production run, and even using air shipping, I don't think that cases will arrive to us before some time early January, and maybe mid-January if there are any delays due to holidays 😢 They advertise lead times 15 days, but that will be a full month now instead...
* **Straps and USB cables are stuck on a train in customs somewhere in Poland.** As you know, the situation in Europe is no good, so the checks for trains coming in are apparently extended or something, I'm not sure. There are no problems with our shipment, just delays, but we don't know when it will continue on its way to us. Probably will arrive way earlier than cases anyway.
* PCBs are still in production. I sent you pictures last week, and production should've been done already, but it looks like manufacturers underestimated how long it would take.

So yeah, we're moving, but slower than expected. **Do we expect all of this to delay Slimes more? Yes. Hopefully not by much. Maybe +a month.** We're doing our best to move things as fast as possible without making rash decisions. Stay updated in <#1025118385585004544>.
