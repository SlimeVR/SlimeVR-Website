Speaking of making a new thing better. **0.13.1 server was released recently!** And then immediately updated with a 0.13.2 fix 😄 A bunch of cool changes and improvements, read more here and don't forget to update: https://github.com/SlimeVR/SlimeVR-Server/releases/tag/v0.13.1

**We also have 0.5.0 version of firmware with magnetometer support on BNO085 and other official slimes!** We're still working on an easy way to update your official slime's firmware, but if you're into DIY like and have BNO085 or official slimes, maybe go try it out? <img src="/blog/emoji/firEyes-785674652755689492.webp" alt="firEyes" class="discord-emoji" /> We'd appreciate feedback on it https://discord.com/channels/817184208525983775/1263850970891157626

**This week we also worked on a dongle for big slimes!** As you might know, smol slimes won't be using wi-fi and will connect to your PC via a dongle that they will connect to. But similar is possible for big slimes too! We're not sure how useful it's going to be, because it's still *almost wi-fi* (ESPNow), but it might be helpful for some people who have problems with their wi-fi routers or network connections.

Right now it's very raw and early to even know if it's a viable thing, but it works and there are a few contributors who want to help finish it. And we're always into supporting more options for the users!
