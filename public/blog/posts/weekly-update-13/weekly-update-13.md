Eyo, !

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #13**, on Tuesday!

Aint much happened this week, it's heatwave here again, and I'm dealing with improving SlimeVR server and figuring out life stuff, also working on looking for other manufacturers if it'll be possible to make SlimeVR in EU or USA. **There is some info I can already share, I guess, but don't take it as final just yet!**

1) Looks like manufacturing in EU is out of the question. Because i would need to pay VAT on import of parts in the EU, which is 20%, and i won't be able to return it, because i'm not selling the stuff directly, so it'll be tariffs with extra steps... Also price of manufacturing there is way way over what's is in China and even in USA.
2) Few USA manufacturers replied to me with their quotes and it looks not promising. I get quotes for PCB assembly that are up to *15 to 20* times higher than in China, and it negates all possible savings on the tariffs, and I would have to work with an unknown manufacturer, instead of the one I already have relationships with.
3) **Some good news though**: Crowd Supply is working on new stuff where they might ship things from Free Trade Zone, which would make so that at least customers outside of US won't need to pay the tariff! Y'all would still need to pay VAT and such, but that was always the case. So there is some good news. It's still work in progress, I'll keep you updated.
