Sorry for the delay! Let's dive into

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #66**

Sadly, will be a bit of a mixed bag. **The chips availability this year looks dire.** Both BNO085 and FSP201... Yes, I was also excited 😢 But we had a meeting with TOP-Electronics (our chip supplier) and it seems like FSP201 will be delayed. So we are trying to figure out what to do next. **We should have a call with CEVA in a couple of weeks where we will solidify our plans for this and next year's chip supply.** So I can tell you more then, but this doesn't mean we don't do anything...

**Here's current plan:**
- CEVA is committed to ship us 32000 of BNO085 till November in batches. This will cover all campaign orders and most orders placed afterwards. So if you already ordered slimes you're probably good to get them till November/December this year.
- We moved planned shipping date for all *future* orders to March 2023. This is a guestimate! We will know more precise info after the call I mentioned.
- It's possible that future orders will be delivered earlier or later, as well as it's possible that some orders placed in the last couple months will be delivered later too. I will be able to say after this call and after I get all orders info from Crowd Supply.
- Our plan is to have all other parts manufactured and ready by the end of September, then we will be assembling slimes as chips come and send them to Crowd Supply. Stay tuned for more info as well. **So basically we are on the "finish line" of production of other things, and will begin assembly and shipping soon without waiting for all chips.**

So yeah, it kinda sucks but there is silver lining. We probably won't get a lot of extra chips to put SlimeVR in stock, but should still get enough to cover our current promises as well as outline how they will be shipped in the future.

- **I and @Tom Yum working on polishing case design.** It's a bit of work, but I hope we can finish it in the next week or so and start ordering molds <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesshaking-662674837558525968.webp" alt="eyesshaking" class="discord-emoji" /> Very excited because I never done anything like this!
