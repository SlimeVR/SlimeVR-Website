Sorry for the lack of an update last time! As I said, we were pretty busy with IRL stuff. So, some news, I guess?

**We now have @cake32 here with the rest of the team in The Cave!** She was the one who designed protection and other improvements for the R14 Main Board PCB, as well as full mechanical and electrical design of our new tester <img src="/blog/emoji/watame_bounce_Nod-854100337090756648.webp" alt="watame_bounce_Nod" class="discord-emoji" /> We're very excited to have her here finally to work on current and future projects that will make VR even better <img src="/blog/emoji/eyeshakebig-1115217029155278888.webp" alt="eyeshakebig" class="discord-emoji" />

**Chain Assembly finished assembly and packing of purple LBS, CS and FBS sets!** So over 1400 sets are already packed and ready to ship to us. We're still packing black and white and DTS sets, but that shouldn't take long. I will update the <#1129107343058153623> sometime next week, stay tuned. We hope to ship S9 in a couple of weeks.

Speaking of <#1129107343058153623>, Mouser has been very slow actually shipping S8 orders to backers... I had a call today with Crowd Supply, and basically they say that the issue is that there is a lot of manual work involved in fulfilling CS orders, and they are working on improving it, etc etc but like... No idea when it will be resolved and shipped. We're still half way through the S8 and they seem to be shipping like ~100 orders per day, so, yeah. At least they said it won't be a problem when Slimes are in stock, but we're still a couple month away from it at least.

Speaking of! **Chain also assembled all of "Batch 3" slimes!** So besides the 1400+ sets they packed, they also have 27000 purple slimes assembled on hand, and they're turning them into S10 now, which will include **all of the current orders so far!** We hope they will finish packing by mid-August, they pack really fast but we're missing stickers (lol), which will be solved in a week or so.

Oh and for the "Batch 4" (future orders and in-stock) we already got the PCBs! We aren't rushing to test and assemble them, because we're busy with the backlog of other tasks, but that will likely be done throughout August, so in early September they can start assembling 30k more slimes! <img src="/blog/emoji/eyeshakebig-1115217029155278888.webp" alt="eyeshakebig" class="discord-emoji" />
