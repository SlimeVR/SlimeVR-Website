@eiren finally flew to Denmark for a well-deserved vacation, for the first time in three years, and today I am writing an update <img src="/blog/emoji/Heart-1117832451620868136.webp" alt="Heart" class="discord-emoji" />

**「Shipments progress and numbers」**

Shipment 9 delivered to Mouser and already being send to people! <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" /> Some of you should start getting their slimes very soon!

This week we received straps for Shipment 11+, so our warehouse is again filled with pieces of slimes. We updated the straps and added a anti-slip silicone backing and double stitching! <img src="/blog/emoji/bagelnom-1053418848671502357.webp" alt="bagelnom" class="discord-emoji" />
**「Updates and work in progress」**

We are also almost finished working on the warehouse tracking system, a huge thanks to @Futura for his work, this will help deliver and store slimes in a more organized and efficient way, yeah yeah, we did not find a ready-made system that would suit us and wrote our own, we hope to publish it in open source one day.

New projects are on the horizon and most of the team is working on them in the cave: look at what a cool case prototype @snaila made for smol slimes, *they are sooo small!!! * <img src="/blog/emoji/catdatanom-877903520815280128.webp" alt="catdatanom" class="discord-emoji" /> Very soon we will be ready to announce even cooler and more convenient things for tracking, and maybe even haptic feedback, we hope to pleasantly surprise you.

We are also actively working on improving the experience for the onboarding 🛳️ process on the server side, so that the software is more convenient and understandable to use. Also, a team of contributors is working on a new version of firmware for slimes, which can significantly improve the experience of their use, and we in the cave are catching up with them, finishing the ability to update the tracker's firmware just from the app! <img src="/blog/emoji/bingus-1157717351861596200.webp" alt="bingus" class="discord-emoji" />

Also we working with @Smeltie on updates in video 📼 format . A few weeks ago we started filming videos, and even went to Chain Logistics to film the slime assembly process by our partners, and of course we will tell you about what has been happening over the past few years in video format.

Next week @eiren will be back and we will return to a more familiar format of updates from them. <img src="/blog/emoji/snailHi-720166013458448385.webp" alt="snailHi" class="discord-emoji" />
