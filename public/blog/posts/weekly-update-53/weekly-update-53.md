Okay, ! There are some good and not good news in today's

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #53**

First of all I got more stuff! All ordered PCBs arrived - BNO085 modules (precious <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbnom-833469925980110908.webp" alt="pcbnom" class="discord-emoji" />), Main and Extension PCBs. **I tested one, they work great!** That's a huge relief because I was worried if I made any mistakes 😅 Some pics attached, way more in <#907246949005148170>, check them out <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesleft-804681881752305685.webp" alt="eyesleft" class="discord-emoji" />

I also received the cases, so now I can try proper PCB in a proper case! It works great and weights less than Tundra <img src="/blog/emoji/blobsquee-711842986589290576.webp" alt="blobsquee" class="discord-emoji" /> I'm pretty sold on the side loops, I think I may stick with them for official slimes, but some live testing required. Extension case needs a couple more iterations, it doesn't close tightly.

I also have a bunch of extension wires!

**Now to the bad part of all of this...**

As you know, we've moved to Estonia a a while ago. But a few days ago, Estonia decided to not extend our stay due to the war that is going on, essentially putting us on timer for how long we can stay here. That is obviously not a good situation to live in, essentially waiting for either things to change, or being departed. Many people here have been hit by this policy change, but we don't want to wait. **So we are moving!** Like, right now. Second time since the crowdfunding campaign end <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_a-847203539352551544.webp" alt="nya_a" class="discord-emoji" /> Not gonna say where until we're officially there, but still Europe.

What that means is that, **it's hard right now to predict what the next month will be in terms of production progress**. Most complicated part is moving all tools, printers, machines, and many parts that we already have on hand. We can still do a lot of work, but until we find a new place where we can put all things and set up a new workshop, physical building will be complicated or impossible.

Last time it took us a few months to finally settle in a new place and get back to speed, but this time I hope we can be done by the end of May. This will also give other contributors plenty of time to work on the features that will make SlimeVR the best it can be! <img src="/blog/emoji/blobsquee-711842986589290576.webp" alt="blobsquee" class="discord-emoji" />

**How dose it affect timeline?**

- Review sets are pushed until June most likely.
- First batch of CrowdSupply orders still depend on chips availability, which I don't have a timeline yet.

So yeah... 😅 It's gonna be okay!
