**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #12**, !

This is exciting stuff! **Yesterday I released the SlimeVR Server** for people who DIY-ed SlimeVR trackers to test! Aaaaaaa!!! After a few bug fixes, it works, and @Torikari was the first to test it out with 5 trackers, and showed us all in voice chat! That was really exciting and nerve-wracking for me XD People seem to like it, yay, makes me incredibly happy! Once again, thanks to everyone of you for being such an amazing enthusiastic community! <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_hearts-812127555443490836.webp" alt="nya_hearts" class="discord-emoji" /> <img src="/blog/emoji/holoheart-787771219776372786.webp" alt="holoheart" class="discord-emoji" /> <img src="/blog/emoji/slimenom-663823059920224277.webp" alt="slimenom" class="discord-emoji" />

I made a video recording of the call, and will do a cut some time soon with fun moments for y'all :3

The server is still early work in progress, some more updates will be out before the trackers get to reviewers. Which they should soon, at least the trackers for Mysticle are in his country already!

In some other news: **I'm investigating moving production from China to USA or Europe, which should eliminate USA import tariffs**, and bring SlimeVR price back to lower. Stay tuned for more info when I know more. Pre-orders are so close, I'm very excited!

On the less fun news is **chip shortage is at it again**: SlimeVR shipping might have to wait some time due to it :( No concrete info yet, but most likely November shipping will not happen...

On another note, please check info about recent small wave of spammers from Adrian above and stay safe!
