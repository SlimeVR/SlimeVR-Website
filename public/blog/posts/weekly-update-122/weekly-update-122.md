## FCC Stuff

The testing that I was worried about was successfully finished. The lab found power values within allowed limits and they aren't much lower than default, so there will be no effect on connection or anything like that. We will have new firmware version with them next week, and will start testing it internally and when sending warranty replacements.

There is still some stuff to be done. The lab didn't say that they finished all the tests, so we're still waiting for them. There is also a bit of documentation, we need an FCC representative in the USA (which we hope Mouser / Crowd Supply will help), and finish user manual updates.

New labels are also on order and we won't re-label, we will just put new labels in the boxes, because whatever <img src="/blog/emoji/firL-785677220093231134.webp" alt="firL" class="discord-emoji" /> I know some companies do that, and that's what others suggested too, so it's fiiiiine, right? <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_a-847203539352551544.webp" alt="nya_a" class="discord-emoji" />

## Assembly

We cleared up the cave from pallets of S4 and moved them into storage for now in anticipation of S3 coming back soon. More slimes are being packed, we have a whole shelf still left to pack, can't say how many yet, counting isn't my strong side 😄

We also will send ~2000 slimes to the assembly company hopefully next week for them to start assembling, in the meantime we will continue soldering, testing and packing.

### New Tester

I finally got to work on the new tester. It required a bit of <img src="/blog/emoji/CatKnifeuwu-1117499569765351464.webp" alt="CatKnifeuwu" class="discord-emoji" /> work with a knife to fit the new board into the old hardware, but I could confirm that the new board works. Not sure yet if it works better, will need to reprint all the base parts next week so it had proper support and proper contact with the slimes to do proper testing. But at least this very compicated design finally tested and works!
