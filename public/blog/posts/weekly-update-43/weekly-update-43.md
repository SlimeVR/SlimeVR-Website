**OUR BNO085 SUPPLIER RECEIVED THE FIRST BATCH OF CHIPS (1000 pcs) FROM THE MANUFACTURER AND WILL BE SHIPPING THEM SOON TO ME!** <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesshaking-662674837558525968.webp" alt="eyesshaking" class="discord-emoji" /> First chips shipment on the timeline is a good start!

As I mentioned before: first shipment will be for testing, review and other internal usage, it's improbable that any of the Slimes we'll make from them to go to backers (not the last of the reasons is that there is a lot of stuff to be done left before everything is ready to ship). And it can't be put into production right away anyway for two reasons: PCB design is not validated and finalized yet; some other chips are waiting to be received.

On the same note: **PCB manufacturer they have received some of the other chips** (TP4056 and ESP-12F) and should receive others soon, but they will get CH340N only in March. This may or may not cause delays of the review batch I mentioned before, because I might just order the same components in another place for the first batch.

That's still very exciting, parts are starting to come in <img src="/blog/emoji/slimepcbnom-839133114931347486.webp" alt="slimepcbnom" class="discord-emoji" />

ANOTHER EPIC THING: **WE HAVE A GOOD CANDIDATE FOR PCB MANUFACTURER IN EUROPE!** I don't want to mention the company until we actually agreed and worked together. I am also waiting for replies from a few others. There is a slight problem though - I already ordered $50k USD worth of chips (see above) to our Chinese PCB manufacturer <img src="/blog/emoji/notheh-820155473839128596.webp" alt="notheh" class="discord-emoji" /> So idk yet how we will handle it, hopefully they can ship it all to me for a fee...

**What does production in Europe means for backers though?** Not much. The price and promised quality will stay the same, but it does a few things for me: the logistics should be easier; and I should save many $$$ despite the fact that manufacturing in Europe costs much more - mostly because I won't need to pay the USA import tax from China. And saved money is a big deal - it means that the project is easier to pull off if something unexpected like chips price increase happens, it also an investment into the future - next batch of Slimes and maybe future projects! (Also can afford to live which is also nice).

I can't wait for all this <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesshaking-662674837558525968.webp" alt="eyesshaking" class="discord-emoji" />
