With not that many news tbh. As mentioned above, **UPS was supposed to pick up S7 yesterday, but they just didn't come.** I emailed them in the evening, but likely it was already the end of the work day. I hope they can pick it up on Monday. If you want to follow the shipment when it's picked up, the tracking link is here: https://scsapps.ups.com/forwardinghub/us/en/tracking/tracksearch?searchBy=1&trackingNumber=3261064147

This week was mostly working on new tester design, packing warranties, wrapping up the S7. We started pre-packing S8 and further while we don't have parts. We're also in talks with Crowd Supply for them to put another order of Slimes that we aim to cover all the sales for this year, so we could get slimes in stock eventually <img src="/blog/emoji/firPog-785701297478959104.webp" alt="firPog" class="discord-emoji" /> Since our sales are growing, we need to keep ordering parts in advance to catch up <img src="/blog/emoji/firL-785677220093231134.webp" alt="firL" class="discord-emoji" /> And ofc get more help.

Next week we know that at least new extension cases and half of the new main cases will ship to us. Also extension PCBs should be finished next week, and half of the ordered BNO085 modules.

We should also be sent samples of the assembled main boards. We decided it'll be way safer for everyone if we get production sample from the manufacturer and check both the design and the quality before they make 56000 main boards. Besides, they're still waiting for the rest of BNO085 to be shipped to them, and finishing extension boards.

Next week will likely be start of the new tester build. We're also working on a small VR space in The Cave, and we're now coming up with new ideas on how to make it better and make new cool stuff :3 Right now we're working on a DIY charger for the slimes, and if it works out we'll release the design for everyone to use (and maybe we might sell the end product, we'll see...)
