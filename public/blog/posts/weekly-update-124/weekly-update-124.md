## New Tester

I also finally **finished the new tester** this week! It's mostly an update to the tester's PCB by @cake32, but also some minor mechanical and software changes to it. But that's a very huge thing overal, because testing speed greatly increased - updated tester is way more reliable and consistent! In fact, @Az retested main PCBs that we had before (3500+) in just two days! We have ~10000 more soldered and ready for testing, so I hope it will be pretty quick to go through them and send to the assembly company :3 They're waiting for the parts <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbnom-833469925980110908.webp" alt="pcbnom" class="discord-emoji" />

### New Firmware

We needed to re-test the PCBs because we are uploading new firmware version to them! **Firmware 0.4.0 is finally stable and out** and it will be on all new slimes! We needed to do that updated because of the changes we had to do to the firmware after FCC testing, we had to lower transmitter power by ~10-15% to be compliant. In the future, Sever will have a way for you to update the firmware over Wi-Fi, but the feature is still in development! Overall, 0.4.0 doesn't affect official trackers much, since most of the changes are for DIY trackers, but if wanna check it out, please do!

## New Main Board Revision

**We got the Revision 14 main board samples!** They have IMU integrated on the board, which will help to speed up assembly of the slimes a lot, since we won't need to solder them. It also have a bunch of anti-static protections, that hopefully will reduce the amount of slimes breaking. **I hope we can put them on order next week or the week after**, just need a few minor adjustments, and get the rest of the main boards by the end of the year. Currently we have enough main boards to cover old batch 1 and batch 2, and the rest we still need to order, but we've been working on the new revision, which is now ready!

## Protective Plugs for Extension Port

Another thing that will improve reliability and logevity of the slimes, including old revision, are protective plugs for extension port. We're hard at printing tens of thouthands of them! That will take a bit, but thankfully we don't need all of them at once, and our printer can print 1000 at a time in ~9 hours! Check the time-lapse 😄 We might get another printer honestly... Bambus are just too good.
