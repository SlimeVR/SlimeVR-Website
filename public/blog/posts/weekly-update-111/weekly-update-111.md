1) All broken slimes, missing extensions, broken or missing cables or other parts will be replaced. Right now we ship extensions and extension cables right away. For the main trackers we're waiting on packaging for shipping, which should arrive tomorrow, and will start shipping replacements too.
2) We are investigating the reasons for the issues and how the slipped past the tester and what we can do to minimize them in the next shipments. We've done a bunch of investigations at the Cave and by the community. That's how we got this extreme overclocked dry ice-cooled slime, for example ❄️ (pics attached) <img src="/blog/emoji/firL-785677220093231134.webp" alt="firL" class="discord-emoji" />
