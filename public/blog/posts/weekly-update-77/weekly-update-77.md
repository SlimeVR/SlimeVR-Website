Here TheMysticle explains how: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IbD9YwQ1wds Yes, it's all very experimental, but thanks to work of many people, including some who just joined in the last week, we're getting there! <img src="/blog/emoji/firPog-785701297478959104.webp" alt="firPog" class="discord-emoji" /> Standalone future is looking bright!

* **PCBs finished production!** And are already on their way to our warehouse! After they arrive, in roughly two weeks, we will start part of the assembly <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesshaking-662674837558525968.webp" alt="eyesshaking" class="discord-emoji" /> Can't wait!

* It looks like we found a nice manufacturing space here in Rotterdam :3 Still finalizing formalities, but soon we will start building our office/production space <img src="/blog/emoji/firPog-785701297478959104.webp" alt="firPog" class="discord-emoji" /> I never had anything like this, that would be so cool.
