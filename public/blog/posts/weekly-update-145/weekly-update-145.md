No pictures today, and the news are pretty short but exciting!
## Assembly
We sent parts for 15000 trackers to the assembly company! We will likely send ~10000 more by the end of the week too.

S8 is being packed, but we aren't done with it yet, our focus was on testing the PCBs for the assembly and making the second tester. We need to keep the assembly company occupied as our main priority <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbnom-833469925980110908.webp" alt="pcbnom" class="discord-emoji" />
## Second Tester
Second tester is almost done. We faced a problem that PCBWay sent us panels that weren't CNC'd to our tolerances 😢 So the assembly is pain, but we hope to finish it early next week and double our testing capacity!
## Parts for the orders
We have most of the parts required for the next 56000 slimes. We're waiting on set boxes for the assembly company to pack them, they're in production right now.
## We have a new place!
The Cave is moving! <img src="/blog/emoji/firPog-785701297478959104.webp" alt="firPog" class="discord-emoji" /> We're relocating ~1km from our current place in a place that's 50% bigger! That'll be very helpful, since we outgrown our current place. The new one will have way more office & workshop space for all the new people that are coming in June and July, but a smaller warehouse. Since we won't do much assembly ourselves now, that's an ideal arrangement.

The new place is laid out way better too, so we could arrange our work much nicer, so stuff like long fulfillment of the online store orders, or warranties happens less often <img src="/blog/emoji/eyeshakebig-1115217029155278888.webp" alt="eyeshakebig" class="discord-emoji" /> This is very exciting! But the move would have to wait... As we're busy with S8 & S9, and the new place also needs some work before it's ready (walls need to be painted, and floors replaced lol <img src="/blog/emoji/nihilismnom-416893163018387457.webp" alt="nihilismnom" class="discord-emoji" />)
