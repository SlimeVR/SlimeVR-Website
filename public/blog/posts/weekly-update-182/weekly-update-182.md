Next, of course you want to know about **Shipment 10 and Shipment 11**. Due to issues on CS's dashboard, I can't do a fresh export, it works only sometimes and the last one was yesterday. But either way, during the holidays Mouser's warehouse is mostly not operational, so don't expect a lot of shipments today or even next week. As far as I know, they come back to work mostly on January 2nd, but they'll likely have a lot of other stuff to catch up. **So we hope the pace will return on the week of January 6th. And hopefully even with automation!**

**Shipment 12 was not shipped to Mouser yet.** Literally everyone at The Cave is on vacation, and UPS won't work much this week anyway, so we didn't do anything for it yet. I might do all the paperwork next week, but it'll likely ship on the week of January 6th too. It should reach Mouser pretty fast, and then it all depends on their new systems.

As I said, **all of The Cave is on vacation this week and the next one!** We all need the deserved rest and I hope you understand :3 During this time, support will be slow, and shipment of orders from our parts store will happen on January 7th when PostNL picks it up on schedule (with a slim possibility that something will ship on Jan 3rd). THIS IS NOT RELATED TO SHIPMENT OF SLIMEVR SETS, only our Shopify parts store!

Meanwhile, the rest has been very helpful and we're already full of new cool ideas that we hope to implement next year! <img src="/blog/emoji/firPog-785701297478959104.webp" alt="firPog" class="discord-emoji" />For now I will keep it to ourselves, but we're very excited! <img src="/blog/emoji/eyeshakebig-1115217029155278888.webp" alt="eyeshakebig" class="discord-emoji" /> <img src="/blog/emoji/slimepcbnom-839133114931347486.webp" alt="slimepcbnom" class="discord-emoji" />

But I will leave you with a couple of photos of a few prototypes we're tinkering with!

First of all, new glove PCBs, because the wires soldering got very annoying. I'm very excited to tinker with it next year <img src="/blog/emoji/firPog-785701297478959104.webp" alt="firPog" class="discord-emoji" /> It's a complicated product, and this is just a proof of concept, but it's fun to play with, and maybe something will come out of it.

And the other are test PCBs of SlimeVR with different IMUs! All of the promising ones :3 Now, we don't want to get ahead of the horse, the development of new firmware, fusion algorithms and ways to evaluate them are still ongoing, but it's true that BNO is not going to be the king of fusion forever, so having prototypes on hand to play around and evaluate is a good thing <img src="/blog/emoji/nod-1279885450194194506.webp" alt="nod" class="discord-emoji" />

That said, if you're worried about drift, there are some other cool things in development that everyone will be able to use, regardless of what their IMU is! Like magnetometer toggle for BNO, or even new drift compensation algorithm that is currently being beta tested, they're super-promising 👀
