Smort ppl in <#912982617010352159> is testing and improving support for MPU-9250 and ICM-20948 <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesshaking-662674837558525968.webp" alt="eyesshaking" class="discord-emoji" /> Thanks to @ThreadsOfFate, @mafuyu and @unlogisch we now have ICM-20948 support in main firmware! Testing and fixes are continuing <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" />

It's a simple start/stop button for now, but it's a great start for making SlimeVR something much more than just FBT for SteamVR <img src="/blog/emoji/blobsquee-711842986589290576.webp" alt="blobsquee" class="discord-emoji" />

It doesn't cost an arm yet!
