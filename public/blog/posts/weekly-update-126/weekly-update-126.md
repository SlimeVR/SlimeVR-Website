If we waited, we wouldn't be able to finish certifications until holidays and then who knows when, and it would've been another huge delay, so we had to pay <img src="/blog/emoji/aki_flop-908156245079363594.webp" alt="aki_flop" class="discord-emoji" /> At this point I'm just desperate tbh. The rest is done, as soon as it's ready we should be fine to submit all the documents to FCC.

## Shipment 3

We have Shipment 3 back. It looks fine, survived the travel well. FCC only opened one set of slimes and did it pretty carefully, no damages. So yeah, the process of re-flashing and re-packing is started!

## Re-packing

We've been organizing re-packing this week! We have re-organizied our Cave to have more space and moved our work places upstairs before the winter hits (it's chilly downstairs) and just generally sorting stuff out and organizing the process.

Besides putting new labels on the slimes, there is a bunch of stuff we need to do with them, actually... The most annoying is that all slimes have to be updated to firmware 0.4.0 (at least in theory)... For that we made an updater, that can over USB connecte slimes to wifi, update the firmware over wifi, clear wifi settings, and also do self-test on the slimes to make sure they didn't break while in shipment and storage! It's pretty fast and one PC can do 10 slimes in parallel, but it's still a lot of work, and we really want to be safe to not have any problems again with anyone.

We're making a small update line for everything: unpacking, flashing, new label, putting extension plugs in, packing new extension wires, and packing boxes back. It's a process, but we should go full throttle on it next week, since everything is already here!

## Assembly

Parts for almost 4000 slimes (not sets, main slimes) were sent to our assembly partner, and we already have soldered and tested parts for 4000 more, and finishing testing for a few thouthand more. As soon as we're done with the rest, we will also ship it to them. We and they think all of it can be assembled before the end of the year.

For now our focus is on what we already have, as well as re-packing S3 and S4. That said, we have prepared A LOT of boxes with accessories and stuff for when we have more main slimes to send more!

It's been a really busy week here in the cave, and next one will be even more busy. FCC should be submitted, and slimes should start being packed for shipment (ofc the actual shipment would have to wait until FCC is done).

So yeah. Have a nice weekend, I'm dead tired, so see ya next week.
