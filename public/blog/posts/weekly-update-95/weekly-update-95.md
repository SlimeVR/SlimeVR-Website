**The box is moving**, we're fixing last details, sent payments to the manufacturer and they're ready to print as soon as we're ready.

We have the new tester PCB, but it has an error <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbheh-943141381310926858.webp" alt="pcbheh" class="discord-emoji" /> I spaced the testing wrong and the second part of the panel doesn't align (pic attached). The new one is on the way... Meanwhile, we're working on the new testing jig design.

Also, as you know, we have shipped 25 DIY Kits some time ago. It's a small thing, but it gave us some important info. **Some of the slimes that we sent had defects.** Thankfully because of this we were able to get them back (and send replacements), figure out the defect (broken MOSFET), and we will update our testing procedures with the new tester that will also test the MOSFET in question, as well as a few other things that we were missing. Sadly, as a result **all the boards we already tested will need to be retested.** 😢 That is not a big of a problem, since they don't need to have firmware flashed, which was ~90% of the testing time, and the new tester will do a fast job out of them all, but that's still a small hurdle. We also had to disassemble the handful of trackers that we already assembled qwq. I think that's better than shipping someone broken trackers though.

On the good news, **@Az has been soldering so many trackers per day that we're almost out of un-assmbled first batch main boards** <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_a-847203539352551544.webp" alt="nya_a" class="discord-emoji" /> He do be fast, lol. I will be ordering second batch main boards as soon as we get new samples in a few days.

So, yeah. Grind is going!
