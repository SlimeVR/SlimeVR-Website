Hemlo, ! <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" /> We're taking a New Year break this week, so the assembly (and streams!) will return next week. Anyway, today we have some news about the last week!

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #82**

You might have seen some news in <#907246949005148170>, we are assembling and testing SlimeVR Main boards at a nice pace! Tester was improved, soldering process was improved, and we even caught a few soldering errors and dead boards with it <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" /> That's exactly why we go though all of this, since even factory-made PCBs can have some fails or dead chips, and our tester should catch them. Thankfully, failure rate rn is extremely low, we tested over 500 boards with only 3 failures that are possibly from manufacturing or dead components (we will investigate each failure in detail next week before continuing the testing).

**Tune in next week for our stream**, we will have a different composition next time - our second printer is gone along with @Smeltie (it was their printer) who returned home for now (we miss you <img src="/blog/emoji/Heart-839953810432589884.webp" alt="Heart" class="discord-emoji" />) You can find us at https://youtube.com/@SlimeVR We will hopefully also have better stream quality, since we should get better internet next Monday too!

It seems like **USB Cables train was unstuck!** Supplier tells us that the cables should be with us in a few days, so hopefully the same will happen with the straps soon too. If not, we will be ordering straps again with air shipping if nothing moves till the end of the week (we're waiting for a quote from manufacturer, it's holidays, as you might know...)

**Purple case samples will be sent in 2-3 days!** We asked case manufacturer to make samples for purple cases before production, so they should be here at the start of the next week, VERY EXCITING! <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesshaking-662674837558525968.webp" alt="eyesshaking" class="discord-emoji" /> If all good, everything will go for the full run! <img src="/blog/emoji/V13Case-998973749342777455.webp" alt="V13Case" class="discord-emoji" />

**Order for extension cases is submitted!** It took a bit long, but extension case design is finished, and will be in validation and production soon! Exciting x2! This should go way faster than main case, because we used previous experience to make it, which should reduce back-and-forth with manufacturer on fixes + it's way smaller part. So hopefully it will be here by the end of the month! <img src="/blog/emoji/crossed_fingers_paw-762351521244381235.webp" alt="crossed_fingers_paw" class="discord-emoji" />

Honestly, can't wait to continue, but we need rest (and also I got cold, so need to heal a bit).
