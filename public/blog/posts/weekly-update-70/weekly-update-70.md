Heyo and ! Sorry for the last time, it has been a stressful week. Let's get back to

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #70**!

The most important thing, and why I'm tagging Backers in this update: **There is now a <#1025118385585004544> channel that in detail explains current state of production of SlimeVR trackers** as well as our best estimates of when everything will ship and how many!

There are a few important updates I will need to highlight about it:

- **It is most likely that SlimeVR will ship in January 2023.** So around 2 months change. This time it is partially our fault, not just chip shortages, for which I'm deeply sorry. Even though we have 4000 BNO085 on hand and will soon have 4000 more, we can't ship them right away, because some things are not ready. As you can see in the channel, some stuff was ordered only recently and shipping times for straps are really long, and they most likely will be in our warehouse only by the end of November.

Why we didn't order everything earlier? The war, moving countries, mental and physical health and other such issues, and we just lost track of time and simply failed to realized this is something that should've been done a long time ago. We have also been delaying it because we didn't know if we should order for 2023 batch right away or should wait, but in hindsight we should've just focus on current orders of course. Anyway, **this is our failure and I'm deeply sorry for this error**. We live, we learn, we are ordering things for next batch as soon as we know how much of everything we need and settle everything with Crowd Supply, so next batch shouldn't have any delays.
- Second, **For 2022 we will only get 32000 chips and we don't have confirmations for 2023 yet.** 32000 is how many we ordered initially, and this doesn't cover all current preorders. I can't get enough data from Crowd Supply yet to say when the cutoff is, but I think it's safe to assume that if you ordered slimes after May, you will be getting yours in the next wave - March/April 2023. The confirmation for it should be by the end of October, CEVA is waiting for confirmation from Bosch... <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_flop-847202537425731604.webp" alt="nya_flop" class="discord-emoji" /> And yes, it is as frustrating as it sounds.

So thems the current state... **For this week the plan is** to finish ordering everything we need to order, maybe except case extension as it's still in the design stage (shouldn't delay production). As well as find packaging manufacturer, but that should also be quick production according to all quotes we were given and there is no long shipment time, because we want to manufacture packaging here in Europe.

So yeah. **It's definitely a mess, but with all your support and help of the contributors, we're moving though this mess <img src="/blog/emoji/holoheart-787771219776372786.webp" alt="holoheart" class="discord-emoji" /> Thanks everyone.** Once again, check <#1025118385585004544>, it will be updated right away when there are any changes and new information, and I will highlight the updates in dev updates too.

See ya next time, ask your questions in this thread or in "Production progress thread".
