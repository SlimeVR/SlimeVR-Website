Sup

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #117** here <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" />

First of all, **the assembly and packing continues**. We ran out of IKEA boxes to put assembled and un-packed slimes, so we decided to continue packing them again. So next week you should see the number of assembled sets in S4 or S5 go up <img src="/blog/emoji/upstonks-830622344644001793.webp" alt="upstonks" class="discord-emoji" />

We didn't want to pack them before because we would need to put a new sticker on them after certification is done, but considering that we would need to fix stickers on all slimes in S3 and S4 anyway, and that doing a proper transparent sticker that we use will take a while after we know our new FCC ID, we decided to continue as is and then apply a fix sticker on it...

I know you don't care what sticker slimes have <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_a-847203539352551544.webp" alt="nya_a" class="discord-emoji" /> neither do I, honestly <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_a-847203539352551544.webp" alt="nya_a" class="discord-emoji" /> but as you have seen in all this story, sometimes government cares, and we have to figure out how to do everything as required.

**We also have a company that wants to help us with the assembly**. We got pretty okay-ish preliminary price, and this week we will send them 200 slimes so they can do trials and see how much time and effort it takes, if they will do it or not, and what the final price gonna be. Idk when they will give us the final answer, but I hope this will imrpove our assembling capacities and move most of the manual work out of The Cave.

**On the FCC situation** we kinda have been ghosted by 4 companies and one that were actually replying didn't reply to the last email <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_a-847203539352551544.webp" alt="nya_a" class="discord-emoji" /> If they don't reply to my ping by tomorrow, we're moving this shit to China. There is probably a reason why everyone does certification in China.

**Also we have a lot of BNOs**. Like. Way too many, honestly. This, including the rest we have, should be enough for all current orders. **We're also ordering the rest of the parts for all current orders**, batch 3 and beyond. Currently on order are the batteries and extension cables (V3!). We have most of the straps and cases, so it's not a big priority rn. IMU modules will be ordered soon, I wanna do a quick schematic update. Main PCBs will be ordered after we do a schematic update to improve reliability, for which we're waiting a result of the X-Ray of broken slimes. We're also working on a minor case revision to improve assembly process and maybe reinforce the strap loop (we'll see what's possible without making a whole new mold).
