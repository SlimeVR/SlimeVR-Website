Okay , this time there are some exciting news <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesshaking-662674837558525968.webp" alt="eyesshaking" class="discord-emoji" /> so let's dive in tonight's (again on Thursday!)

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #59**!

**WE HAD A CALL WITH CEVA!** (CEVA is the manufacturer of BNO085, to be precise they put their own firmware into Bosch's BNO055, and their firmware contains a lot of magic) There are some good and bad news. Let's start with bad: there is little hope of getting all required BNO085s this year. Bosch can't supply CEVA with enough chips, and CEVA can't supply us with enough chips. No one knows when it may change, but hope is thin for BNO. <img src="/blog/emoji/bno-968229024805560370.webp" alt="bno" class="discord-emoji" />

**BUT THERE ARE SOME GOOD NEWS TOO!** CEVA is releasing another chip - FSP201. It's not an IMU, it's a microcontroller that contains CEVA's magic, and it works with other IMUs to provide sensor fusion (i.e., the magic sauce that makes BNO085 this good). And along with it they offer an IMU from another manufacturer - ST's LSM6DSR. There are two major things with it: **both of these chips will have good availability** and **together they should perform on par with BNO085!**

Details:
- FSP201 by CEVA and LSM6DSR by ST work together and should have similar performance to BNO085. This remains to be seen when we get hands on samples, but I'm very hopeful.
- CEVA secured stock of both chips for us to cover all of the current orders, increased Crowd Supply order, and then some. Besides, LSM6DSR currently have good availability, and CEVA promises a ton of FSP201s, so there shouldn't be any problem with chips.
- **If all goes well, we should get samples in a few weeks, and will be able to validate performance. Stay tuned.**
- **Full batch of promised chips should be available as expected end of August-September, so just in time for November shipment date.**
- If this really goes well, Slimes might be in stock after this????? <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesshaking-662674837558525968.webp" alt="eyesshaking" class="discord-emoji" />
