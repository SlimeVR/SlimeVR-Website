Meanwhile, it seems that **Mouser finally done with the Shipment 8**. Most of the orders that were supposed to ship, shipped, baring some still pending updates or filling export documentation, or such. So if you didn't get your email from Crowd Supply yet, you're likely in S9.

They have almost everything they need besides a few extensions, and should be done soon. 👀 S10 should be faster to ship after it's packed, because it'll be just purple slimes, so less paperwork and pallets stacking for us. I hope we can ship it in just a couple of weeks!
