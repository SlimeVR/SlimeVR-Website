I won't do @ everyone today, just & , but this is an important one!

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #35**

**I got some news from the IMU chip supplier.** Chip shortages keep on giving... Due to the fact that Bosch raised their prices, and due to inability to produce enough chips in 2022, **shipment of most of the chips was moved to the end of September 2022.** Also they increased prices per chip, but this won't affect y'all. And yes that's somehow legal and normal in this decade :(

**If the supplier's schedule is to be believed (which I'm not too hopeful at this moment):**
- **There will be a small batch of chips in February.** They will be used for testing and promotions. If any left over they may go to some of the first backers.
- **There will be bigger batch of chips (8 000) in June.** It will go to directly to the first backers.
- **Rest (over 20 000 chips) will arrive by the end of September** and will go to the rest of the backers and people who placed orders later.
This timeline might change because of changes the from supplier and also because we are still negotiating shipping details.

**So the shipment date of most SlimeVR sets will have to be moved to November 2022.** This includes all sets ordered now, all sets ordered after campaign end, and most of the sets ordered during the campaign. Shipment date on the campaign page and all orders will be updated as soon as Crowd Supply gets to it.

**We are still talking details with the supplier, the manufacturer and Crowd Supply.** I want to make sure this doesn't happen again, and if we should order more chips to cover additional orders of SlimeVR for 2022. After all this settles, I'll @ everyone and post an update about this and other stuff on Crowd Supply campaign page. I just thought it's a good idea to let everyone know what's going on as soon as possible. Especially considering that people take their time to reply, it's better to let it out ASAP.
I'll create the thread to this post if you want to express your (fair) frustration. I am once again incredibly sorry, I got the info as soon as possible. **If the new timeline doesn't work for you at all, feel free to contact Crowd Supply to cancel your order here:** https://www.crowdsupply.com/contact/change-order No hard feelings, I understand, and you will be fully refunded.

Another thing that happened was that IMUs increased in price by 20%. On the scale of current production it's ~$35k USD... Luckily there is enough safety margin in the project budget that it won't affect my ability to produce SlimeVR, but <img src="/blog/emoji/notheh-820155473839128596.webp" alt="notheh" class="discord-emoji" /> it's no fun. I was planning to put these money for a better use...

To stop this from happening with other parts, **I'm ordering all other chips this month.** They are better off sitting in the warehouse, than increase in price to the point where project becomes unsustainable. I'm discussing this with our PCB manufacturer right now, will have more info in the next update.

If you're canceling orders or just want something now, **you can try DIY'ing your trackers!** There have been a lot of progress in this regard lately, but sadly price of BNO085 breakout boards also increased to absurd in the last couple months. But we are working on adding support for a lot more other IMUs, and MPU-6050 is a viable option as you can see by @ZRock35 videos in <#903962635161174076>. Either way, if you're interested, join <#823930029070876736> and check our docs site https://docs.slimevr.dev/.

There have been some progress updates, but I'll leave it for the next time, this is long as it is.

Stay safe. And once again, thanks for your support <img src="/blog/emoji/luvuwu-842594708161298484.webp" alt="luvuwu" class="discord-emoji" /> See ya soon.
