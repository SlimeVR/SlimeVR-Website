**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #1!**

- I have new test cases! See pictures below. We are working on design and comfort with a friend, and it feels and looks better now. At least test/review version will have two loops for straps, because it's easier to print and it's sturdier. For release version we will be trying more things, including clip, plastic and metal one. Current print is a bit bigger than required, because test board is bigger, but it still feels kind of nice. I also ordered a 3D printer for myself, so I will be going through more prototypes than I could do bothering a friend :D

- Release Candidate board design is ready, and I'm waiting ansers from manufacturer to order them! I will be ordering 25 for all sensor chips I have (curse the chip shortage aaaa), and they will go to reviewers and such. I'm bummed I can't make more rn and send it to more reviewers and people from the community, but that's completely out of my control right now :( I don't know who will get them, first I will get them myself, test them that everything is fine, etc etc etc... It also means that board design is ready! Another friend helped me imrove the design, including better power delivery and efficiency, better parts choice, and other hardcore electrical engineering stuff I didn't have experience with, so this is going to be epic ^^ Finished board is just 4x4 cm! Smol! It can be made smaller ideally, but battery will be 4x4 too.

- Speaking of electrical engineering! I did a lot of experiments, upgraded firmware, and asked another friend to poke with her oscilloscope in my prototype boards, and we figured the reason for sensors crashes that I complained couple of weeks ago. It was not great power delivery on the board. So it should be good, because new board have much better power, and the prototype version that doesn't crash also has similar power circuit. Basically, it's a rambly way of mine to say that everything will be fine, I asked! You can check some voltage spikes and electronics stuff on the pics :3
