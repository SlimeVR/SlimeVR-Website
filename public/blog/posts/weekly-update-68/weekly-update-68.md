We are already pressing them for next year's order, ordering 70k more chips, and they say it will be in 2023 Q1, but... you've seen the trend. So until they're actually shipped, it's hard to say. They and Bosch are very unreliable, but there is literally nothing else good on the market as of now <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbheh-943141381310926858.webp" alt="pcbheh" class="discord-emoji" />

What that means that **some later orders will be moved to March-April or something like that, including all new orders**. I will do calculations a bit later where the expected cut-off is going to be, but that's how things are right now. I'm personally sorry for this and, frankly, not sure what I could've done differently, but still. Stupid chips, stupid war '>_<'
