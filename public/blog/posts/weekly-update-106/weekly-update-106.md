Today's **Dev Mondays-ish Updates #106** is the second most important one (after the start of the campaign one)! THEY'RE ON THE WAY TO BACKERS!!! <img src="/blog/emoji/blobsquee-711842986589290576.webp" alt="blobsquee" class="discord-emoji" /> We're so happy about it you wouldn't believe 😭 We're thankful so much to everyone who helped, who waited, who believed, who are still waiting and supporting us <img src="/blog/emoji/Heart-1117832451620868136.webp" alt="Heart" class="discord-emoji" /> **THANK YOU!!!** Thank you and <img src="/blog/emoji/Heart-1117832451620868136.webp" alt="Heart" class="discord-emoji" />, especially people in the cave. And everyone else, of course! You're the best community anyone can wish for 😭 (happy tears)

As mentioned before and in <#1025118385585004544>, this shipment only has 1414 sets, and only tiny bit of them are 5+3 and 7+3, because of how we started packing and assembly... That's our fault and we're sorry, we're working on the next shipment to happen very-very soon, sometime mid-July. **It's not all, but it's a huge step and there are many more steps but now the path is known and the rest should be way easier!** <img src="/blog/emoji/Heart-1117832451620868136.webp" alt="Heart" class="discord-emoji" />

As I said before, it should take probably couple of weeks for Slimes to reach Crowd Supply, due to legal BS we have to ship first two batches through Estonia (since that's where we started the company originally), so it'll take a bit of time, but they're on the way! <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesshaking-662674837558525968.webp" alt="eyesshaking" class="discord-emoji" />

We're taking the rest of the week of to celebrate and unwind, so there won't be the next update probably, unless something happens important. There will be an update on Crowd Supply page, and I will post again when the slimes leave Estonia to go to Crowd Supply properly, and when they arrive too.

After this, our plans are to improve our assembly process. We're getting better, but it's a bit of a mess, now we have more space since 4 pallets are out, and we have more ideas, so it should improve. **And we intend on shipping slimes regularly without making big batches, for example, ship them monthly.** Of course, we will fix the assembly order issue and will assemble more in line with how people ordered, so early backers will receive slimes earlier! **We will know more after everything arrives and people start getting slimes, so thanks for your patience, we ask you for a little bit more!** <img src="/blog/emoji/Heart-1117832451620868136.webp" alt="Heart" class="discord-emoji" /> We have a lot of orders to catch up, we'll do our best to do it as fast as possible without dying <img src="/blog/emoji/blobsquee-711842986589290576.webp" alt="blobsquee" class="discord-emoji" />
