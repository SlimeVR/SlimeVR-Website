## We have the first box of Main PCBs!
Like mentioned above https://discord.com/channels/817184208525983775/818062236492759050/1232643028229816360, UPS delivered us the fist box out of 50 with the Main PCBs for the next batch of slimes. No idea why it was only one, but it looks like the rest will be on Monday or something like that. <img src="/blog/emoji/pcbheh-943141381310926858.webp" alt="pcbheh" class="discord-emoji" />
## We're testing the PCBs!
The new tester is assembled and it's amazing! It tests 20 boards instead of 10, it tests two stages of the testing instead of one, it's way way way more reliable and consistent and easy to use, and it does it only around 50% slower in total. So it ends up saving a lot of time, since it does 4 times more work in 1.5 times more time... Anyhow, it's great and we already tested the box mentioned above! This is the coolest thing I assembled tbh 🥺
## Assembly picking up soon!
Yesterday we shipped most of the parts for 20000 slimes to one of our assembly partners (everything except the PCBs that we don't have yet). This cleared a lot of space in our warehouse <img src="/blog/emoji/firL-785677220093231134.webp" alt="firL" class="discord-emoji" /> But also they're ready for the assembly.

20000 is way less than we need, sadly, but they only have so much capacity right now. That's why we're talking with other companies about it too! The one we were trying to work with was pretty slow to respond, so we have now contacted two more, and we hope that by spreading the work + making some assembly ourselves, we'd be able to catch up with the backlog soon <img src="/blog/emoji/blobfingerscrossed-586821384718647306.webp" alt="blobfingerscrossed" class="discord-emoji" />
## More parts on order!
We ordered a lot of parts for the future batches! BNO, stickers, screws, boxes, etc for all the slimes are on order to ensure that no delays happen because of that. We will also place an order for more PCBs as soon as we get these and see if we need to make any more changes to them. Long lead-time stuff like batteries, straps and extension cables are already here, so there won't be delays on these.
