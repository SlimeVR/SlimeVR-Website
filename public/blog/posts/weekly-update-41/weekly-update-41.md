Hey gang! Getting back on the schedule <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" />

**Dev Mondays-ish Updates #41** will be short, because the last one was not long ago. Still, here's what happened since:

- Few fixes were released for 0.1.4 mostly fixing backwards compatibility. **Also there is now test support for MPU-9250 with magnetometer, and BMI160 in the latest firmware!** <img src="/blog/emoji/eyesshaking-662674837558525968.webp" alt="eyesshaking" class="discord-emoji" /> Huge thanks to @boronare, @렛삐 and @Emojikage for it. We are on an ongoing quest to find better IMUs for DIY that don't cost absurd amount of money and drift way less than MPU-6050 (which also been increasing in price and getting worse over time...). Check out "IMUs support" if you want to try DIYing your Slimes with other IMU! (This doesn't affect official production slimes). Support for ICM-20948 is coming soon too.
- I started working on "road map" of sorts and overall improvements to project management here: <https://github.com/orgs/SlimeVR/projects> it's still a work in progress. I also organized my own notes and plans.
- Messaged two suppliers in Taiwan about chips, there is no hope they will have anything, but miracles happen...
- Started work on the SlimeVR PCBs I have... They needed some parts from old boards <img src="/blog/emoji/slimepcbnom-839133114931347486.webp" alt="slimepcbnom" class="discord-emoji" /> (see the pic)

**Here's what's in plans for the next week:**
- Update and publish next PCB design with fixes of all current problems and order test samples
- Contact manufacturers in Europe about making these PCBs
- Work more on project management
- Fix my 3D printer and start printing test cases

Hopefully February will be the month when I will know all details about where and how SlimeVR will be manufactured <img src="/blog/emoji/nya_umu-850498715617198080.webp" alt="nya_umu" class="discord-emoji" /> (I know what I was planning to for it, but there might be better options...)
